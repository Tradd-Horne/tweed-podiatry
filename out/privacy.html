Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Tweed Heads Podiatry is a home-visit podiatry service run by Tradd Horne, B.HSc (Pod) (AHPRA POD0001880268, ABN 19 615 606 347). We handle your personal and health information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles, and the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW).

What we collect

  • When you ask for a call back: your name, phone number, suburb, and anything you write in the message box. That can include health details.
  • When you call or text us: your phone number, the time of the call, and the content of any text message. Calls are not recorded.
  • When you become a patient: the details needed to treat you safely — contact details, medical history, medications, assessment and treatment notes, and funding details (Medicare, DVA, NDIS, aged care or private health).
  • When you visit this website: anonymous page-view statistics. Our analytics tool does not use cookies and does not identify you.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and book a visit.
  • To assess and treat you, and to keep clinical records as the law requires.
  • To claim from a funding body you have asked us to bill, and to send reports to your GP or care provider when you agree or the referral requires it.

We do not sell your information. We do not send you marketing unless you ask for it.

Who we share it with

Only the people involved in your care or payment (for example your GP, care provider, Medicare, DVA, NDIS or your health fund), and the services that run this practice for us:

  • Our practice management and clinical records system.
  • Twilio, which carries calls and text messages to our phone number.
  • Cloudflare, which protects this website and the call-back form from spam.
  • Our website and email hosting providers.

Some of these providers may process data outside Australia (for example in the United States). We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to the Australian Privacy Principles. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How we keep it safe

Records are stored electronically on secure, password-protected systems. Only Tradd Horne and anyone working under his direction can see them. We keep health records for at least 7 years after your last visit (or until age 25 for a child), as NSW law requires, and then destroy them securely.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of your records or ask us to correct them. Email contact@tweedheadspodiatry.com.au or call 0495 090 752. We will reply within 30 days.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, contact us first at the email above. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992) or the NSW Information and Privacy Commission (ipc.nsw.gov.au, 1800 472 679).
