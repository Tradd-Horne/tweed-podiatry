Areas visited

Home visits across the Tweed coast, the Tweed valley and the northern end of the Byron shire — from Tugun and Coolangatta in the north down to Brunswick Heads and Mullumbimby, and inland as far as Tyalgum and Uki. Coastal suburbs are grouped onto the same run, so appointments in one area usually sit together on the same day.

Suburbs with their own page

Each one sets out what a visit involves there and how it is funded.

  • Tweed Heads 2485

    The base for this practice, so appointments here are the easiest to fit in — often within the same week.

  • Tweed Heads South 2486

    Between the Tweed River and the Terranora Broadwater, and part of the same daily run as Tweed Heads itself.

  • Tweed Heads West 2485

    On the western side of the Terranora Inlet, and covered on the Tweed Heads run.

  • Banora Point 2486

    A short drive from Tweed Heads, with a large retired population and several villages we visit regularly.

  • Terranora 2486

    Hilly and spread out, which is exactly where getting to a clinic is hardest and a home visit earns its keep.

  • Bilambil Heights 2486

    Up in the hills behind Tweed Heads, where steep driveways and internal stairs make a clinic trip the hardest part of the day.

  • Chinderah 2487

    On the river between the highway and the coast, and covered on the way south to Kingscliff.

  • Fingal Head 2487

    Out on the headland past Chinderah, grouped with the Kingscliff run.

  • Cudgen 2487

    On the red soil ridge behind Kingscliff, and covered on the same coastal run.

  • Kingscliff 2487

    Visited on a set run down the coast road, so Kingscliff appointments usually sit together on the same day.

  • Casuarina 2487

    Between Kingscliff and Cabarita on the coast road, so it sits in the middle of the same run.

  • Cabarita Beach 2488

    Grouped with the Kingscliff and Pottsville coastal run. Bogangar is the same locality and is covered with it.

  • Hastings Point 2489

    Between Cabarita Beach and Pottsville on the coast road, and grouped with both.

  • Pottsville 2489

    At the southern end of the coastal run, so Pottsville visits are grouped onto particular days.

  • Murwillumbah 2484

    Inland from the coast, and covered on the same day as the surrounding valley.

  • Uki 2484

    In the valley under Wollumbin, and covered on the same day as Murwillumbah.

  • Burringbar 2483

    On the Tweed Valley Way between Mooball and Stokers Siding, and close to the centre of the area we cover.

  • Ocean Shores 2483

    At the southern end of the area, above Brunswick Heads, and grouped with it.

  • Brunswick Heads 2483

    The southern edge of the run, visited on the same day as Ocean Shores and Mullumbimby.

  • Mullumbimby 2482

    Inland from Brunswick Heads, and covered on the same southern run.

  • Coolangatta 4225

    Just over the border, and close enough to Tweed Heads that it is part of the standard run.

  • Tugun 4224

    The northern edge of the area, a short run up the coast from Coolangatta.
