Services

Comprehensive podiatry services delivered to your home with the same professional standards as a clinic visit.

General Foot Care

A full foot health assessment — circulation, sensation, skin and nails — then the nail care, callus and corn treatment that follows from it.

Ingrown Toenails

Assessment and treatment of ingrown toenails. If the nail needs surgery, that is assessed first and arranged separately.

Diabetic Foot Care

Specialised assessments and ongoing care for patients with diabetes to prevent complications.

Sports Podiatry

Assessment and treatment of sports-related foot and lower limb injuries. Biomechanical analysis included.

Custom Orthotics

Prescription orthotics tailored to your feet to address pain, improve function and enhance comfort.

Paediatric Podiatry

Specialised care for children's feet, addressing developmental concerns and growing pains.

About Your Podiatrist

Tradd, B.HSc Pod is a qualified podiatrist bringing professional foot care directly to your home across Tweed Heads, the Northern Rivers and Northern NSW.

With experience across multiple clinical settings, Tradd takes a patient-centred approach, assessing the entire body from the ground up using contemporary methods to deliver lasting value.

Mobile podiatry removes the barriers of travel, parking and clinic waiting rooms. Whether you have mobility challenges, a busy schedule, or simply prefer the comfort of home, quality podiatric care comes to you.
