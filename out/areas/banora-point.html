Home visit podiatrist in Banora Point

Podiatry at home in Banora Point 2486. A short drive from Tweed Heads, with a large retired population and several villages we visit regularly.

The drive to Banora Point

Banora Point spreads back from the Tweed River behind Barneys Point bridge, with Chambers Lookout above it. It has one of the largest retired populations in the shire, and several of the villages here we visit on a regular round.

Banora Point is 6.9 km from our base in Tweed Heads, about 11 minutes each way — roughly 22 minutes in the car before we have seen anybody. That is close enough that we can usually fit a visit in at short notice, often in the same week.

If you are near Chambers Lookout, Oxley Park or Jenner's Corner Park, you are inside the area we visit.

On the same run

Banora Point is grouped with Chinderah, Tweed Heads South and Fingal Head, so those visits sit on the same day. If someone else near you needs a visit as well, mention it when you ring and we will put you together on the one run.

What a visit in Banora Point covers

A full assessment comes first: pulses and circulation, sensation and nerve function, skin and nail condition, and how the foot is loading when you walk. Then the treatment — thickened nails reduced, corns and hard skin removed, pressure points relieved — and a look at your footwear if it is working against you. All of it in the comfort of your own home, with everything needed brought along.

Paying for it

$170 for a first visit to Banora Point and $150 after that, with no call-out charge on top — Banora Point is inside the area we cover. A Home Care Package, a DVA card, an NDIS plan or private health extras all change what you actually pay. Every price is set out on the fees page.
